About KROMA Academy

Educating Visual Storytellers Since 2012

Founded by studio animators and VFX supervisors, KROMA bridges the gap between creative passion and global production pipelines.

01 / Story & Vision

Built by Artists, For Artists

KROMA began as a boutique 12-seat workshop dedicated to teaching real production Maya and compositing workflows. Today, our multi-floor creative campus houses motion-capture stages, render farms, and dedicated UI/UX design labs.

Our Philosophy: Technique without storytelling is hollow; storytelling without craft stays unseen. Every student masters both.

Our Mission

Empower creators with studio-grade skills and fearless portfolios.

Our Vision

Be the launchpad for the next wave of animation & interactive directors.
